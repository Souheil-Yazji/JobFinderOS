# Wins Library — TEMPLATE

> Copy to `config/wins.md` (gitignored — your private data). This is the heart of the
> **Progressive Profiling** system: a growing library of your real professional wins,
> captured *beyond* your résumé. The agents draw on these to write tailored cover
> letters, application answers, and outreach grounded in real evidence instead of
> generic claims.
>
> You build this two ways:
> 1. **`/profile`** interviews you to add and deepen wins over time.
> 2. **Passive capture** — when you correct a draft or mention a story mid-task, the
>    agents append it here automatically.
>
> A "win" works for any career: a teacher raising reading scores, a nurse cutting
> triage time, a marketer lifting conversion, an engineer shipping a hard system.

## How each win is structured
Use STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) with a quantified result wherever possible.
Tag each win so the agents can match it to a role or interview question.
